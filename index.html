Privacy Policy for VibePlayer

Last updated: October 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

VibePlayer ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how VibePlayer operates as an offline media player application for Android devices.

2. OFFLINE OPERATION & DATA COLLECTION

VibePlayer is a 100% offline audio player. We DO NOT collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, user identification, listening telemetry, or audio files to external servers or third parties. All your audio files and metadata remain strictly on your local device.

3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS & PURPOSE

To function properly, VibePlayer requests the following specific permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used exclusively to scan your device storage for local audio files, retrieve track metadata (such as title, artist, album, and artwork), and render your music library.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used strictly to display playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip, Track Info) in the Android notification shade and lock screen during active audio sessions.

4. LOCAL DATA STORAGE

Information generated by your use of the app—including playlists, favorite songs, recently played history, and app preferences—is stored locally on your device using encrypted Android DataStore and Room SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

VibePlayer does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that harvest personal information.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

VibePlayer is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, children's privacy is fully protected.
